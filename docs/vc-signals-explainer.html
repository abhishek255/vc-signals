How the weekly radar works

The important idea: pain and chatter are useful, but they do not automatically become company rows. They first become themes. Then the system searches for companies that credibly map to those themes.

1. Source collectionPull recent signal from Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub, social/video sources, and grounded web search when configured.
↓
2A. Pain and theme evidenceReddit complaints, HN discussions, developer pain, market chatter. This can create a theme, not a company row.
2B. Candidate evidenceCompany pages, HN launches, GitHub repos, funding pages, founder/product pages. This can become a radar row.
↓
3. Company Discovery From ThemesIf the system sees repeated pain like "AI agent permissions," it runs targeted searches such as "AI agent security startups Seed Series A founder launch."
↓
4. Rank and explainEach qualified company/project gets Investment Interest, Evidence Confidence, Why On Radar, Why This May Be Noise, and a suggested next action.
↓
5. Read-only Attio awarenessThe brief checks whether Marathon already knows the company, whether it is stale/no owner, or whether it was previously passed. It does not write to Attio.
↓
6. Weekly partner artifactPartner Review, Full Radar, Sector Intelligence, Themes With No Company Yet, Company Discovery From Themes, weak evidence summary, and audit JSON files.

What each section means

Partner Review

The 10-15 rows a partner should look at first. These are ranked for interest, not just evidence volume.

Full Radar

Up to 50 qualified rows. No filler. Includes stage, raised, headcount, founders, LinkedIn, X, Attio URL, OSS score, and best source when trusted evidence exists.

Themes With No Company Yet

Useful market pain where the system does not yet have enough company evidence. These are research prompts, not fake company rows.

Company Discovery From Themes

The second-pass searches created from those themes. This is the bridge from pain signal to investable company discovery.

Sector Intelligence

For every requested sector, the brief says whether it found companies, OSS projects, pain without companies, source failures, or no meaningful signal.

Weak Evidence / Rejected Summary

Shows what was filtered out and why, so quiet sectors are explainable instead of mysterious.

Market Sector is not Source Lane

This distinction makes the radar easier to read. A row can be about Cybersecurity even if the evidence came from GitHub.

How sources are used

Candidate-eligible by default

Grounded company pages, HN Show/Launch posts, GitHub repos, funding/company pages, and clear founder/company pages can become radar rows.

Mostly supporting evidence

Reddit is mainly used for curated pain discovery. TikTok, Instagram, Threads, and YouTube help only when the company/product identity is clear and corroborated.

Read-only CRM context

Attio is used to match by domain/name and add status, owner, last touch, stale/no-owner, passed-company flags, and CRM URL. It does not write records.

Why output can be OSS-heavy

Honest caveat

If a grounded web search key is missing, the system has a harder time finding company websites, funding pages, founder pages, and LinkedIn-style company evidence. GitHub and HN remain easier to verify, so the weekly radar may skew toward OSS projects. The brief labels this instead of inventing weak company rows.

Scoring model

The radar keeps interest and confidence separate. That lets Marathon see promising weak signals without confusing them for fully verified opportunities.

Investment Interest

How interesting the opportunity is if the evidence holds: timing, market pain, technical specificity, wedge, freshness, and fit for Seed to Series B.

Evidence Confidence

How much the system trusts the evidence: source quality, company identity, recency, corroboration, founder/company links, and CRM context.

What it does not do yet

It does not replace judgment

It accelerates judgment by surfacing evidence, gaps, and next actions. Partners still decide what deserves a meeting or deeper diligence.

It does not write to Attio

CRM integration is read-only for now. Future Slack or Attio writeback can be added later, but the current product is deliberately conservative.

It does not treat LLM guesses as evidence

The agent-native research workbench can suggest possible companies to verify, but those leads stay out of the canonical radar until a real source supports them.
